# Controlled Handoff

## To QA & Certification

Review the unmerged remediation pull request, execute positive and negative validation, assess public-data safety and ownership boundaries, and issue an independent finding. Do not infer certification from Engineering completion.

## To FederationOS Command Center

Record the Engineering disposition and route consultation to Security Governance, Records & Evidence Custody, Registry & Metadata, PMO & Release Governance, Runtime Operations, and Repository & Platform Authority. Do not designate the repository canonical or authorize merge solely from this Engineering handoff.

## Recommended sequence

QA, Security, Registry, Custody, PMO, and Runtime review may proceed against the same immutable pull-request commit. Repository & Platform Authority should re-verify after required platform controls and authority conditions are addressed. Merge requires separate competent authorization.
