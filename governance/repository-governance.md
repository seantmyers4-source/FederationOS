# Repository Governance Candidate

Changes must use branches, traceable commits, pull requests, independent review, evidence references, and controlled merge authorization. The intended `main` controls are protection, required PRs and checks, CODEOWNERS review, force-push and deletion restrictions, controlled merge methods, and attributed bypass.

Engineering cannot verify or configure all administrative settings through the available connector. Those controls are requirements, not assertions of enabled state. Until independently enabled and verified, the compensating control is an unmerged pull request and explicit prohibition on direct substantive changes to `main`.

Canonical designation remains with the Repository & Platform Authority. Publication, certification, release, and activation remain with their respective authorities.
